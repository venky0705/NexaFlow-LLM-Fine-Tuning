This controlled source document defines NexaFlow Technologies requirements for support priority and sla. This document applies to support agents, support managers, technical teams, and customers whenever work falls within the support priority and sla domain. It should be read together with relevant security, privacy, contractual, employment, financial, product, and legal requirements. ## Policy and operating information
1. Priority 1 incidents are incidents that make a production service broadly unavailable or create a severe security or data-integrity risk.
2. Priority 2 incidents cause major degradation for important functionality without complete service unavailability.
3. Priority 3 incidents affect limited functionality or have a reasonable workaround.
4. Priority 4 requests cover general questions, minor issues, and feature guidance.
5. Enterprise Priority 1 cases have a target initial response time of 30 minutes when 24-hour critical support is included.
6. Business Priority 1 cases have a target initial response time of 2 hours during covered support hours.
7. Target response times are service objectives and do not guarantee resolution within the same period.
8. Support priority may be adjusted when investigation shows that the actual impact differs from the initial report.
9. Support Priority and SLA activities must follow documented NexaFlow procedures and applicable legal, contractual, security, and privacy requirements.
10. Customer Operations owns the baseline standard for support priority and sla and reviews material exceptions.
11. Requests or changes related to support priority and sla should be recorded when they affect customers, company data, access, money, employment, or production services.
12. Personnel must use least-privilege access and only information reasonably required for support priority and sla activities.
13. Material decisions involving support priority and sla require an accountable owner and enough documentation for later review.
14. Exceptions to the normal support priority and sla process require documented approval unless an emergency process applies.
15. Sensitive information encountered during support priority and sla must follow data-classification and access-control requirements.
16. Where a customer contract specifies stricter support priority and sla requirements, approved contractual terms take precedence for that customer.
17. Support staff must verify customer identity before security-sensitive changes related to support priority and sla.
18. Support records for support priority and sla should contain enough detail for another authorized agent to understand the history.
