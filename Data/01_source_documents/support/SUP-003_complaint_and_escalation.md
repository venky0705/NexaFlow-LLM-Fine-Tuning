## Purpose
This controlled source document defines NexaFlow Technologies requirements for complaint and escalation. It is designed to give a clear company reference for consistent customer assistance, case handling, escalation, communication, and service quality. The document is intentionally explicit about what is required, what is merely permitted, where approval is needed, and how uncertainty should be handled so that employees and company systems do not invent policy details that are not supported by an approved source.

## Scope
This document applies to support agents, support managers, technical teams, and customers whenever work falls within the complaint and escalation domain. It should be read together with relevant security, privacy, contractual, employment, financial, product, and legal requirements. A customer contract or applicable law may impose an additional or stricter requirement for a specific situation. When that happens, the stricter or specifically applicable requirement should be followed without rewriting the baseline NexaFlow policy for unrelated cases.

## Policy and operating information
1. Customers may request escalation when a support case has significant unresolved business impact.
2. Support managers review escalations involving repeated service failures or unresolved Priority 1 and Priority 2 incidents.
3. Billing complaints involving disputed charges are routed to Billing Operations.
4. Privacy complaints involving personal data are routed to the Privacy function.
5. Security reports involving suspected vulnerabilities or unauthorized access are routed to Security.
6. Support agents must not promise compensation unless they have documented authority to do so.
7. Escalation records should document the issue, impact, actions taken, and current owner.
8. Closed complaints are retained according to the applicable records-retention schedule.
9. Complaint and Escalation activities must follow documented NexaFlow procedures and applicable legal, contractual, security, and privacy requirements.
10. Customer Operations owns the baseline standard for complaint and escalation and reviews material exceptions.
11. Requests or changes related to complaint and escalation should be recorded when they affect customers, company data, access, money, employment, or production services.
12. Personnel must use least-privilege access and only information reasonably required for complaint and escalation activities.
13. Material decisions involving complaint and escalation require an accountable owner and enough documentation for later review.
14. Exceptions to the normal complaint and escalation process require documented approval unless an emergency process applies.
15. Sensitive information encountered during complaint and escalation must follow data-classification and access-control requirements.
16. Where a customer contract specifies stricter complaint and escalation requirements, approved contractual terms take precedence for that customer.
17. Support staff must verify customer identity before security-sensitive changes related to complaint and escalation.
18. Support records for complaint and escalation should contain enough detail for another authorized agent to understand the history.
