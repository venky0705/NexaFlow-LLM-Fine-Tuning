## Policy and operating information
1. Restricted NexaFlow or customer data must not be entered into an unapproved external AI service.
2. Employees must use approved AI tools and configurations when company information is involved.
3. An AI tool must not be used to bypass product access controls, security controls, privacy requirements, or contractual restrictions.
4. Before using an external AI service for business work, personnel should confirm that the service is approved for the intended data classification and purpose.
5. AI-generated output used for material business decisions requires appropriate human review.
6. Prompts should contain only the minimum company or customer information necessary for the approved task.
7. AI outputs must not be treated as authoritative company policy when the model lacks a supporting controlled source.
8. Suspected exposure of Restricted information to an unapproved AI service must be reported through the appropriate security or privacy process.
9. Employees must not use personal AI accounts to store or process Restricted company information.
10. When policy does not specify whether a particular AI use is permitted, the user should seek guidance from AI Governance, Security, Privacy, or the relevant policy owner.
