## Policy and operating information
1. NexaFlow may offer a 14-day self-service trial for eligible Starter, Pro, and Business prospects.
2. Trial eligibility may be limited when an organization has recently used the same trial offer.
3. Sales representatives must not promise product capabilities that are not generally available or contractually committed.
4. Custom discounts above 15 percent require approval from an authorized sales manager.
5. Enterprise proposals may include negotiated pricing, support, security, and implementation terms.
6. Verbal commitments are not a substitute for an approved order form or contract.
7. Sales opportunities containing unusual privacy or security requirements should be reviewed by the appropriate specialist.
8. Customer-facing pricing statements must identify whether applicable taxes are included or excluded.
