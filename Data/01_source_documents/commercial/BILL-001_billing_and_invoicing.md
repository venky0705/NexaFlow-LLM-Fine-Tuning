## Policy and operating information
1. Monthly subscriptions renew automatically each month unless cancelled before the next billing date.
2. Annual subscriptions renew automatically each year unless cancelled before the renewal date.
3. Standard self-service subscriptions are normally paid by card or another supported electronic payment method.
4. Approved Business and Enterprise customers may use invoice billing.
5. Standard invoice payment terms are 30 calendar days from the invoice date unless a contract states otherwise.
6. Duplicate charges and confirmed billing errors can be reviewed for reimbursement.
7. Billing administrators can download invoices from the customer administration portal.
8. Past-due accounts may have selected paid features restricted after the applicable notice process.
