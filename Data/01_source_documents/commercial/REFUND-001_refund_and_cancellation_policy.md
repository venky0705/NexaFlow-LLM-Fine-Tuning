## Policy and operating information
1. An initial annual subscription purchase is eligible for a full refund when the request is submitted within 14 calendar days of purchase.
2. Monthly subscription charges are normally non-refundable after the billing period begins.
3. Automatic annual renewals are normally non-refundable unless required by law or contract.
4. Duplicate charges are eligible for correction after verification.
5. Confirmed NexaFlow billing errors are eligible for correction or reimbursement.
6. Enterprise customers follow the refund and termination terms in their signed contract.
7. Cancellation stops future renewal but does not automatically create a refund entitlement.
8. Customers should submit refund requests through the billing-support channel.
