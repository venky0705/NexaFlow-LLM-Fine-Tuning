## Purpose
This controlled source document defines NexaFlow Technologies requirements for remote and hybrid work. It is designed to give a clear company reference for employment practices, employee experience, manager responsibilities, and fair application of people processes. The document is intentionally explicit about what is required, what is merely permitted, where approval is needed, and how uncertainty should be handled so that employees and company systems do not invent policy details that are not supported by an approved source.

## Scope
This document applies to employees, managers, and People Operations whenever work falls within the remote and hybrid work domain. It should be read together with relevant security, privacy, contractual, employment, financial, product, and legal requirements. A customer contract or applicable law may impose an additional or stricter requirement for a specific situation. When that happens, the stricter or specifically applicable requirement should be followed without rewriting the baseline NexaFlow policy for unrelated cases.

## Policy and operating information
1. NexaFlow uses a hybrid and remote-friendly operating model.
2. Employees assigned to a hybrid contract may normally work remotely up to three days per week with manager agreement.
3. Fully remote contracts are not subject to the three-day hybrid limit.
4. Employees working remotely must use approved methods to access confidential company systems.
5. Confidential conversations should not be conducted where unauthorized people can reasonably overhear them.
6. Company information must not be left visible in unattended public workspaces.
7. Remote work arrangements may be reviewed when role requirements or security risks materially change.
8. Cross-border remote work requires advance review because tax, employment, immigration, and security obligations may apply.
9. Remote and Hybrid Work activities must follow documented NexaFlow procedures and applicable legal, contractual, security, and privacy requirements.
10. People Operations owns the baseline standard for remote and hybrid work and reviews material exceptions.
11. Requests or changes related to remote and hybrid work should be recorded when they affect customers, company data, access, money, employment, or production services.
12. Personnel must use least-privilege access and only information reasonably required for remote and hybrid work activities.
13. Material decisions involving remote and hybrid work require an accountable owner and enough documentation for later review.
14. Exceptions to the normal remote and hybrid work process require documented approval unless an emergency process applies.
15. Sensitive information encountered during remote and hybrid work must follow data-classification and access-control requirements.
16. Where a customer contract specifies stricter remote and hybrid work requirements, approved contractual terms take precedence for that customer.
17. Managers should apply remote and hybrid work rules consistently and escalate unusual employee-relations cases to People Operations.
18. Employee records created through remote and hybrid work must be limited to authorized personnel with a business need.

## Exceptions and escalation

The record should state the reason, scope, duration where relevant, and any compensating action. Urgency, seniority, customer pressure, convenience, or a verbal statement from an unrelated person does not by itself establish an exception. If the policy is silent or two controlled sources appear to conflict, the matter should be escalated rather than resolved by guessing.
