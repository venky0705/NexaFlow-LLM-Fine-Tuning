## Purpose
This controlled source document defines NexaFlow Technologies requirements for employment and probation. It is designed to give a clear company reference for employment practices, employee experience, manager responsibilities, and fair application of people processes. The document is intentionally explicit about what is required, what is merely permitted, where approval is needed, and how uncertainty should be handled so that employees and company systems do not invent policy details that are not supported by an approved source.

## Scope
This document applies to employees, managers, and People Operations whenever work falls within the employment and probation domain. It should be read together with relevant security, privacy, contractual, employment, financial, product, and legal requirements. A customer contract or applicable law may impose an additional or stricter requirement for a specific situation. When that happens, the stricter or specifically applicable requirement should be followed without rewriting the baseline NexaFlow policy for unrelated cases.

## Policy and operating information
1. Standard full-time employment at NexaFlow uses a six-month probation period unless the employment agreement states otherwise.
2. Employees receive written employment terms describing role, compensation, working arrangement, and applicable conditions.
3. Managers are expected to define role expectations during onboarding.
4. Probation reviews consider role performance, collaboration, policy compliance, and agreed objectives.
5. Employment decisions must follow applicable employment law and approved People Operations procedures.
6. Changes to contractual working hours require written approval and documentation.
7. Employees must keep required payroll and contact information reasonably current with People Operations.
8. Confidential employment information is accessible only to authorized personnel with a business need.
9. Employment and Probation activities must follow documented NexaFlow procedures and applicable legal, contractual, security, and privacy requirements.
10. People Operations owns the baseline standard for employment and probation and reviews material exceptions.
11. Requests or changes related to employment and probation should be recorded when they affect customers, company data, access, money, employment, or production services.
12. Personnel must use least-privilege access and only information reasonably required for employment and probation activities.
13. Material decisions involving employment and probation require an accountable owner and enough documentation for later review.
14. Exceptions to the normal employment and probation process require documented approval unless an emergency process applies.
15. Sensitive information encountered during employment and probation must follow data-classification and access-control requirements.
16. Where a customer contract specifies stricter employment and probation requirements, approved contractual terms take precedence for that customer.
17. Managers should apply employment and probation rules consistently and escalate unusual employee-relations cases to People Operations.
18. Employee records created through employment and probation must be limited to authorized personnel with a business need.
