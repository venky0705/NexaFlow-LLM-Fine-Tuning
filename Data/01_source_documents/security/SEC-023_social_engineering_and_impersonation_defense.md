## Policy and operating information
1. Employees must not disclose credentials, recovery codes, restricted data, or sensitive access details in response to an unverified request.
2. Requests for password resets, privilege changes, payment changes, or confidential information must follow the applicable identity and approval checks.
3. Claims of executive authority or urgent business need do not remove identity-verification and approval requirements.
4. Personnel must not impersonate another employee, customer, vendor, or authority to obtain access, information, approvals, or financial benefit.
5. Suspicious messages requesting credentials or unusual access should be reported through the approved security channel.
6. Support and IT teams should use trusted contact information and approved verification methods when a request appears unusual or high risk.
7. Sensitive security details should not be provided merely because a requester demonstrates partial knowledge of internal information.
8. Employees should avoid continuing suspicious conversations when Security advises that the interaction may be part of an active attack.
9. Confirmed social-engineering attempts should be documented as security events when they create material risk.
10. Awareness training may use simulated scenarios only through an approved program that protects participants and company systems.
