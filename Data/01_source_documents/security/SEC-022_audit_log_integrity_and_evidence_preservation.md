## Policy and operating information
1. Security and audit logs must be protected from unauthorized alteration or deletion.
2. Access to sensitive logging systems must follow least-privilege and approved privileged-access controls.
3. Personnel must not modify or remove logs to conceal actions, bypass review, or interfere with an investigation.
4. When an investigation may require evidence, relevant records should be preserved using approved methods.
5. Evidence handling should maintain enough context to understand the source, time, responsible handler, and material investigative action.
6. Material investigative actions and decisions should be documented so that an authorized reviewer can reconstruct the response.
7. Log retention must follow the applicable retention standard and any legal, contractual, or incident-specific preservation requirement.
8. Suspected log tampering or unexplained gaps in security evidence must be reported to Security promptly.
9. Access to investigation evidence must be limited to personnel with a legitimate need for the information.
10. Evidence must not be copied to personal accounts, personal storage, or unapproved external services.
