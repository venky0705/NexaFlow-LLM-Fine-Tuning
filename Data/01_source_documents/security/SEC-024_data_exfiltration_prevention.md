## Policy and operating information
1. Company and customer information must be transferred only through approved systems and for legitimate business purposes.
2. Restricted data must not be copied to personal email, personal cloud storage, personal devices, or unapproved removable media.
3. Bulk data exports require appropriate authorization and must respect product, privacy, contractual, and access-control requirements.
4. Users must not split, disguise, rename, compress, or otherwise alter data transfers for the purpose of evading security controls or monitoring.
5. Unusual or unauthorized data-transfer activity should be reported to Security and investigated through approved procedures.
6. External sharing must be limited to the minimum information required and to recipients authorized to receive it.
7. Data needed for approved support or engineering work should remain in approved workspaces whenever practical.
8. Temporary copies of sensitive data must follow the same classification and protection requirements as the original information.
9. Data exports created for legitimate work should be deleted or retained according to the applicable business and retention requirement after the task is complete.
10. Security controls intended to prevent unauthorized data transfer must not be disabled or bypassed without an approved exception.
