## Policy and operating information

1. Full-time NexaFlow employees receive 30 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
2. Employees joining during the calendar year receive prorated annual leave according to their start date and applicable law.
3. Up to five unused annual leave days may be carried into the following calendar year.
4. Carried-over annual leave should normally be used by 31 March.
5. Public holidays are not deducted from annual leave entitlement.
6. Annual leave requests require approval from the employee's direct manager.
7. Requests for five or more consecutive working days should normally be submitted at least two weeks in advance.
8. Sick leave is managed separately from annual leave.
