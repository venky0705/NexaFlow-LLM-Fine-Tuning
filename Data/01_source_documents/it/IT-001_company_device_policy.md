## Purpose
This controlled source document defines NexaFlow Technologies requirements for company device policy. It is designed to give a clear company reference for reliable and secure use of corporate technology, devices, software, identities, and support services. The document is intentionally explicit about what is required, what is merely permitted, where approval is needed, and how uncertainty should be handled so that employees and company systems do not invent policy details that are not supported by an approved source.

## Scope
This document applies to employees, contractors, managers, and IT whenever work falls within the company device policy domain. It should be read together with relevant security, privacy, contractual, employment, financial, product, and legal requirements. A customer contract or applicable law may impose an additional or stricter requirement for a specific situation. ## Policy and operating information
1. Employees in roles requiring a managed computer are provided a company-managed laptop.
2. Company-managed laptops must use approved full-disk encryption.
3. Supported security updates must be installed within the required update window.
4. Employees must not disable endpoint-security controls without written approval from Corporate IT or Security.
5. Lost or stolen company devices must be reported as soon as possible.
6. Company devices must be returned when employment ends unless an approved written exception exists.
7. Sensitive company information should not be stored on unapproved removable media.
8. Corporate IT maintains an inventory of company-managed devices.
9. Company Device Policy activities must follow documented NexaFlow procedures and applicable legal, contractual, security, and privacy requirements.
10. Corporate IT owns the baseline standard for company device policy and reviews material exceptions.
11. Requests or changes related to company device policy should be recorded when they affect customers, company data, access, money, employment, or production services.
12. Personnel must use least-privilege access and only information reasonably required for company device policy activities.
13. Material decisions involving company device policy require an accountable owner and enough documentation for later review.
14. Exceptions to the normal company device policy process require documented approval unless an emergency process applies.
15. Sensitive information encountered during company device policy must follow data-classification and access-control requirements.
16. Where a customer contract specifies stricter company device policy requirements, approved contractual terms take precedence for that customer.
17. Records created through company device policy should identify the request, owner, date, approvals, and outcome where relevant.
18. The responsible function periodically reviews company device policy procedures for effectiveness.
