## Policy and operating information
1. Company work should use approved corporate storage and collaboration services rather than personal storage accounts.
2. Restricted company or customer data must not be stored on personal USB devices, personal cloud drives, or personal email accounts.
3. Removable media used for approved business purposes must meet applicable encryption and device-management requirements.
4. Unknown or untrusted removable media must not be connected to company devices.
5. Employees should request an approved transfer method from IT when normal corporate tools do not meet a legitimate business need.
6. Personal backups of company information are not permitted when they bypass approved retention, deletion, or access controls.
7. Loss of approved removable media containing company information must be reported promptly to IT and Security.
8. External media should contain only the minimum information necessary for the approved task.
9. Business data copied temporarily to approved removable media should be removed when the approved purpose is complete unless retention is required.
10. Exceptions require documented approval from the responsible IT or Security owner.
