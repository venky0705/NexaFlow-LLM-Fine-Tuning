## Policy and operating information
1. NexaFlow's mission is to help organizations coordinate work, automate repetitive processes, and make operational decisions using reliable software and responsible AI.
2. NexaFlow's vision is to make enterprise automation understandable and accessible to organizations of different sizes.
3. Customer trust is treated as a product requirement rather than only a communication goal.
4. Security by design is expected for new products and significant product changes.
5. Employees are encouraged to document decisions that materially affect customers, security, compliance, or operations.

12. Personnel must use least-privilege access and only information reasonably required for mission vision and values activities.
13. Material decisions involving mission vision and values require an accountable owner and enough documentation for later review.
14. Exceptions to the normal mission vision and values process require documented approval unless an emergency process applies.
15. Sensitive information encountered during mission vision and values must follow data-classification and access-control requirements.
16. Where a customer contract specifies stricter mission vision and values requirements, approved contractual terms take precedence for that customer.
17. Records created through mission vision and values should identify the request, owner, date, approvals, and outcome where relevant.
