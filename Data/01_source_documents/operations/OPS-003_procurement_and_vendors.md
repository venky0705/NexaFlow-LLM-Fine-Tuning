## Purpose
This controlled source document defines NexaFlow Technologies requirements for procurement and vendors. It is designed to give a clear company reference for reliable internal operations, spending controls, facilities, continuity, and vendor coordination. The document is intentionally explicit about what is required, what is merely permitted, where approval is needed, and how uncertainty should be handled so that employees and company systems do not invent policy details that are not supported by an approved source.

## Scope
This document applies to employees, managers, Finance, Facilities, and Operations whenever work falls within the procurement and vendors domain. It should be read together with relevant security, privacy, contractual, employment, financial, product, and legal requirements. A customer contract or applicable law may impose an additional or stricter requirement for a specific situation. When that happens, the stricter or specifically applicable requirement should be followed without rewriting the baseline NexaFlow policy for unrelated cases.

## Policy and operating information
1. New vendors handling Confidential or Restricted information require appropriate security and privacy review before use.
2. Purchases above 5,000 euros require documented budget-owner approval.
3. Purchases above 25,000 euros require Finance review in addition to budget-owner approval.
4. Employees must disclose material conflicts of interest involving proposed vendors.
5. Vendor contracts must be stored in the approved contract-management system.
6. Renewals should be reviewed before the contractual cancellation deadline.
7. Critical vendors should have an identified internal business owner.
8. Procurement records should document the business purpose, owner, cost, and applicable approvals.
9. Procurement and Vendors activities must follow documented NexaFlow procedures and applicable legal, contractual, security, and privacy requirements.
10. Corporate Operations owns the baseline standard for procurement and vendors and reviews material exceptions.
11. Requests or changes related to procurement and vendors should be recorded when they affect customers, company data, access, money, employment, or production services.
12. Personnel must use least-privilege access and only information reasonably required for procurement and vendors activities.
13. Material decisions involving procurement and vendors require an accountable owner and enough documentation for later review.
14. Exceptions to the normal procurement and vendors process require documented approval unless an emergency process applies.
15. Sensitive information encountered during procurement and vendors must follow data-classification and access-control requirements.
16. Where a customer contract specifies stricter procurement and vendors requirements, approved contractual terms take precedence for that customer.
17. Records created through procurement and vendors should identify the request, owner, date, approvals, and outcome where relevant.
18. The responsible function periodically reviews procurement and vendors procedures for effectiveness.

## Exceptions and escalation
A material exception to the normal procurement and vendors process requires authorization from Corporate Operations or another policy owner who is explicitly empowered to approve the exception. The record should state the reason, scope, duration where relevant, and any compensating action. Urgency, seniority, customer pressure, convenience, or a verbal statement from an unrelated person does not by itself establish an exception.
