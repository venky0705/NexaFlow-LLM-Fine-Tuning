## Policy and operating information
1. Employees must use their assigned badge or another approved method when entering controlled NexaFlow office areas.
2. A forgotten, lost, or malfunctioning badge must be handled through the approved Facilities or Security temporary-access process.
3. Employees must not enter a controlled area by following another person through a secure door in order to avoid the badge process.
4. Visitors must follow the approved registration, host, and access requirements for the area they visit.
5. Employees must not lend badges or physical-access credentials to another person.
6. Lost or stolen badges must be reported promptly so the credential can be disabled or replaced.
7. Access to restricted facility areas is limited to personnel with an approved business need.
8. Claims of urgency or seniority do not by themselves create an exception to physical-access controls.
9. Suspicious attempts to enter controlled areas without authorization should be reported to Facilities or Security.
10. Temporary access should be limited in scope and duration to the legitimate need that justified it.
