## Purpose
This controlled source document defines NexaFlow Technologies requirements for privacy and personal data. It is designed to give a clear company reference for lawful and proportionate handling of personal data throughout its lifecycle. The document is intentionally explicit about what is required, what is merely permitted, where approval is needed, and how uncertainty should be handled so that employees and company systems do not invent policy details that are not supported by an approved source.

## Scope
This document applies to employees, service owners, Privacy, and approved processors whenever work falls within the privacy and personal data domain. It should be read together with relevant security, privacy, contractual, employment, financial, product, and legal requirements. A customer contract or applicable law may impose an additional or stricter requirement for a specific situation. When that happens, the stricter or specifically applicable requirement should be followed without rewriting the baseline NexaFlow policy for unrelated cases.

## Policy and operating information
1. NexaFlow processes personal data only for documented and legitimate business purposes.
2. Access to personal data is limited to people and systems with an approved business need.
3. Teams should collect only personal data reasonably necessary for the stated purpose.
4. New processes that materially change personal-data handling require privacy review.
5. Requests from individuals concerning their personal data are routed to the approved privacy-request process.
6. Personal data must not be disclosed to unauthorized recipients.
7. Privacy incidents and suspected unauthorized disclosures must be reported promptly.
8. Where applicable, contractual and legal requirements govern transfers of personal data to service providers.
9. Privacy and Personal Data activities must follow documented NexaFlow procedures and applicable legal, contractual, security, and privacy requirements.
10. Privacy owns the baseline standard for privacy and personal data and reviews material exceptions.
11. Requests or changes related to privacy and personal data should be recorded when they affect customers, company data, access, money, employment, or production services.
12. Personnel must use least-privilege access and only information reasonably required for privacy and personal data activities.
13. Material decisions involving privacy and personal data require an accountable owner and enough documentation for later review.
14. Exceptions to the normal privacy and personal data process require documented approval unless an emergency process applies.
15. Sensitive information encountered during privacy and personal data must follow data-classification and access-control requirements.
16. Where a customer contract specifies stricter privacy and personal data requirements, approved contractual terms take precedence for that customer.
17. Records created through privacy and personal data should identify the request, owner, date, approvals, and outcome where relevant.
18. The responsible function periodically reviews privacy and personal data procedures for effectiveness.
